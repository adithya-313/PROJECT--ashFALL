## Notes on Verified Sources

- **Smartphone Replacement Cycle**: Communications Today names IDC as the research firm ("according to IDC", "IDC Used Device Tracker") and cites "The Hindu BusinessLine" at the end of the report. The figure directly supports the 36-month baseline replacement cycle.
- **Platform Market Share**: Both the canonical URL (`https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/india`) and the specific tracking URL (`https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/india/woehbv.xn--80aacr9ajdjpge1n.xn--p1ai`) resolve to the identical Statcounter Global Stats table for September 2026, reporting Android at 93.89% and iOS at 6.05%.
- **OS Adoption**: MacRumors reports Apple's official App Store transaction data as of February 12, 2026 (published February 13, 2026). The 66% figure is a global metric across all active iPhones; India-specific adoption is not broken out.
- **Festive Season Sales**: The ETTelecom article quotes Counterpoint Research estimating the festive shopping season contributes 20%-25% of annual smartphone sales volume.

## Figures that must stay assumptions

- **Daily baseline phone change rate**: The translation of an average 36-month cycle into a uniform daily probability (~0.09% of citizens per day) and individual variance around this mean.
- **Festival phone change uplift curve**: The precise daily uplift multiplier and wave duration concentrating phone upgrades around festive sales (Counterpoint provides the 20%-25% annual volume contribution, but not a daily hazard rate).
- **App reinstall rate**: The frequency of app reinstalls that trigger a synthetic "new device" signal without a hardware change on genuine accounts.
- **Android OS update rollout and adoption curve**: While Apple publishes global iOS update milestones, Android fragmentation in India spans multiple OEMs and four generations with staggered rollouts. The adoption wave duration, start window, and eligible device fraction remain structural assumptions.
- **Episode frequencies and typology distributions**: Specific breakdown of ordinary citizen episodes across weddings, chit funds, college festival treasurers, loan repayments, and family medical collections, as well as exact duration and inbound transaction velocity distributions.
