## Decisions and Why
- Labels, registry reports and bulk confirmations are built after the run from the finished result, so the runner, the events file and random children 0-16 are unchanged and every frozen anchor holds.
- Label events carry only what a bank could know; truth is joined later by payment_id, so nothing true leaks into the bank side.
- An account counts as confirmed at its first complaint until bank decisions exist (Levels 13 and 22); with that rule, every wrong registry entry comes from a false complaint and no second error rate exists (X8).
- The victim complaint share and the bank reporting share are drawn on separate streams, so changing one never changes the other (L2).
- Ranges are read from the run's layered world_ranges, so stress layers such as complaint-backlog change label delays with no extra code.
- The analyst takes its random draws as inputs, so it stays a pure function until Level 22 wires it to a queue.

## Known Gaps
- The analyst is not called by any run yet; Level 22 connects it to the case queue (12-4 partial).
- A bulk confirmation can confirm a mule still in warm-up, before any fraud money moved through it; the label is true but early. Check 24 shows a batch confirmed after fraud money moved.
- Complaint share is proven by check 25 on 5,000 synthetic losses; check 21's small world (17 victims) proves only the "never happens" rules.
- Confirmation by first complaint is a stand-in for bank decisions; Levels 13 and 22 replace it.
- Accounts opened mid-run have no region, so bulk confirmations never sweep them; their number is reported.
- The bulk-short-lag fixture cannot go through apply_layers (its values sit outside the test ranges on purpose); tests copy its values into a copied effective configuration.
- The bulk planner's number of draws varies with the drawn crackdown count; its stream serves nothing else.
- Short worlds give few complaints and almost no crackdowns, so statistical checks use synthetic inputs or labelled fixtures.
- Carried over: fraud payments are still stamped with the owner's phone; training_devices_retired_on_schedule is still not enforced (12f); the two old lint/type errors remain.
