# Level 12g Handoff Note

Sections "Register Changes" to "Random Stream Children and Tie-Break Order" are generated by `py -3.12 scripts/handoff_facts.py` (signatures from `inspect.signature`, fields from `dataclasses.fields`, keys read from the config files). Regenerate them with that command rather than editing them by hand.

## Where things stand
Level 12g is complete on `feature/level-12g-held-out-families` (from `0574555` to `a7a3827`), merged into `feature/level-12-fraud-world-engine` by pull request. Two held-out families are real (`slow_drip`, `device_rotation`), two are deferred with reasons (`fake_routine_life`, `pseudo_merchant_camouflage`, A-025). Showcase: Level 12 37/37, 28.4s; unit suite 1222 passed, 52 deselected, 7 warnings in 241.54s (0:04:01). Next amendment: A-026.

## Decisions and why
- Held-out behaviour comes from options on existing blocks (route: drip options; take_control: rotation options), each marked `held_out_family` in `config/tactic_blocks.yaml`. The marker is the single source the guard, the truth tag and the validator read.
- The exemption is keyed on the world's split, never its range column; a world with no split counts as training, so a missing split refuses rather than exempts. Plain routes keep the 60-minute and Rs 2,000 lines in every split, so seen gangs in test worlds still behave like training.
- The truth tag is derived from the mechanism: a held-out name without its options is refused, an option under a different name is refused, and one gang may carry only one held-out family, so results stay per family.
- Device ids are numbered across gangs in a world (hotfix); before it, every gang's first phone was `dev_ext_000001`, which faked shared devices across unrelated gangs.
- Config YAML is parsed once per content (SHA-256 of the file bytes) and returned as a deep copy. Profiling showed 874 parses in 2 gang plans; planning became about 63 times faster and the unit suite went from about 13 to about 4 minutes on the owner's laptop.
- `fake_routine_life` is deferred (A-025): building it needs a gang payer funding path that keeps gang money separate from fixture money, and a bug there would corrupt every world. Showcase check 34 therefore shows its deferred status and the rented-mule side it will be compared against, instead of the brief's side-by-side.
- The generator replaces the last gang of every test and stress world with a held-out variant, drawing the family uniformly from the active list; every new draw comes after all existing draws, and all 440 training and validation gang mixes are byte-identical to before (checked on all 440).
- The slow-drip piece cap (5000 per stream) is set from measuring every planned test and stress world (max 2411, world test-b02-0007), doubled for headroom.

## Known gaps
- The slow-drip tail is extreme: the largest stream needs 2411 pieces, and in the worst world drip payments are about 28% of all payment events (median 4% in test, 1.4% in stress). Level 12h's trivial-world check must test whether slow_drip is detectable from payment count alone; if it is, narrow the piece-size range or victims with a stated reason (brief: "a held-out gang is spotted by one feature too easily").
- No held-out family tests the routine-life signal (A-025 (c)); the Level 12h edge case "a mule imitating a salaried employee" is deferred with it.
- Rotation is visible only in device events and truth, because gang payments are stamped with the owner's own device (unchanged from Level 12b).
- Each test and stress world holds exactly one held-out gang, always the last gang; the family split over the 240 worlds is device_rotation 129, slow_drip 111.
- Five modules still read YAML directly and are not cached: `world/pattern_files.py`, `world/layers.py`, `world/account_market.py`, `world/world_sampler.py`, `world/live_mode.py`.
- Old lint and type errors in `world/gang_planner.py`, `world/gang_devices.py`, `world/gang_config.py` and `world/interactive_loop.py` are untouched; counts are in the round reports.
- Truth folders written before truth-v2 are refused by the loader; every world can be replayed from its seed.
