## Decisions and why
- Edge cases are library files with an expected behaviour and a status; a case whose expectation needs a later mechanism records the level that brings it (12-13), and nothing passes on zero instances. Pinned values use the sampled-world nesting rule, not apply_layers, which refuses a pinned range.
- The inflation factor is all complaints per 100,000 successful payments divided by 1.05 (config/base_rate.yaml), a lower bound on the true inflation (A-012). Fraud-off Level 11 cards are unchanged.
- The trivial-world check gates on all 440 training and validation worlds and reports on all 240 test and stress worlds (A-026, partly replacing A-013); its response to a bad report-only result was committed before any run.
- The sanity report compares the world with PaySim genuine rows (balance columns excluded) and IBM AML-Data (HI-Small, DVC-tracked, CDLA-Sharing-1.0); both are synthetic, so agreement shows plausibility, not realism, and nothing is tuned from it.
- The four new showcase checks form part h; the brief's showcase list is mapped to check numbers on the Level 12 page instead of renumbering checks 1 to 37.

## Known gaps
- Measured inflation factors (about 13 to 123 on 20 sampled worlds) sit below the configured target band (training 100 to 2000): worlds hold less fraud than aimed, consistent with scarce labels (about 5 true complaints per world). Recorded, not tuned.
- Trivial-world check: 0 of 440 gated worlds fail (best single signal at most 0.80). Report-only, routine_life separates the held-out slow_drip mules perfectly in 2 of 111 worlds; no change was made (decision in reports/trivial_world/summary.md, A-026 (c)). Routine life remains the signal no held-out family tests (A-025).
- Edge cases 01 (shop volume, Level 15), 08 (trace outage, Level 23) and 13 (recycled number, Level 13) are not yet runnable; 02 is deferred with fake_routine_life (A-025). Cases 09 and 10 share one world episode kind.
- Sanity report differences: the world's amount tail is heavier than PaySim's and lighter than IBM's; PaySim payers almost never repeat. Recorded as-is.
- scripts/handoff_facts.py imports modules by variable name, including evaluation.truth, for signatures only; core.truth_import_check sees literal names only.
- Commit b30aa03 was authored with adithya-313@users.noreply.github.com instead of the usual email; history was not rewritten.
