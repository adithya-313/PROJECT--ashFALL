## Decisions and why
- Sourced recycle delay lower bound: Sourced directly from Supreme Court of India judgment (30 Oct 2023, Civil Appeal No. 13468 of 2021): "the cellular mobile telephone number once deactivated for non-usage or disconnected on the request of subscriber, is not allocated to the new subscriber for at least a period of 90 days." Pinned in `config/nationalsim.yaml` under `recycle_min_days: 90` with label `reported`.
- Recycled number share assumption: Pinned in `config/nationalsim.yaml` under `recycled_number_share: 0.05` with label `assumption`. Reason quoted from yaml: "Assumed share of genuine customer accounts whose assigned mobile number was previously held by a mule account deactivated before day 0; no official industry figure is published."
- Emergent registry coverage (amendment A-028): Coverage by mule depth is measured from Level 12 victim complaints and reporting pipelines rather than being synthetically forced. Reference intervals in `config/world_ranges.yaml` (`registry_coverage_layer1_mule_share` and `registry_coverage_deeper_mule_share`) are displayed beside measured values without clipping or active filtering.
- I4C feed vs DPIP separation: DPIP screening enforces keyed HMAC hashes and bilateral enrollment checks (HTTP 403 Forbidden for outsiders or non-participants). The I4C intelligence feed broadcasts suspect telephone listings globally to all licensed banks regardless of DPIP status, while account listings are confidential to their originating institution.
- Customer disputes and resolution timelines: When a customer holding a recycled phone is falsely flagged by a historical mule report, filing a dispute schedules resolution after `dispute_resolution_delay` (default 60 minutes), updating screening to `match=False, status='resolved'` and logging `minutes_to_resolve` for N4 operational metrics. In contrast, shared household phone disputes linked to active fraud remain `contested`.
- Zero risk score: DPIP Phase-2 transaction scoring is declared out of scope (Revision 2, amendment A-027), so `ScreenResult.risk_score` is strictly fixed at 0.0.

## Known gaps and future work
- Level 21 note: Level 21 introduces operational investigative workflows and alert feedback loops. In Level 21, bank analyst decisions and dispute escalations will make `POST /feedback` calls into `nationalsim/app.py`, which records `FeedbackRecord` entries inside `RegistryCore.feedback` to benchmark false-positive correction rates.
- Remaining Level 13 work: Level 13c will implement baseline forwarding holds and reconcile national registry integration contracts across bank service boundaries.
