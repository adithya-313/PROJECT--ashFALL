## Decisions and why
- Clock protocol and IST time: The event clock protocol provides timezone-aware IST time across ManualClock, ReplayClock, and WallClock implementations. ReplayClock advances simulated time proportionally based on a monotonic source, allowing 1x and 100x replay speeds to evaluate identical event timelines deterministically without drifting or sleeping.
- Blinded HMAC identifier hashing: DPIP fraud registry identifiers (bank accounts, device fingerprints) are hashed with HMAC-SHA256 namespaced by run_id, identifier_type, and bank_index before transmission. Raw account numbers and device IDs are never stored in the national registry mock.
- Listing delay lifecycle: Ingested reports are subject to a configurable listing delay (default 15 minutes, config/nationalsim.yaml). During the delay, screening returns `match=False, status='none'`. Upon reaching `visible_at`, screening returns `match=True, status='listed'`.
- Disputes and revocations: Customer disputes update status to `'contested'` while maintaining `match=True`. Reporting bank revocations deactivate reports so subsequent screening returns `match=False, status='none'`.
- Typed refusal errors: Unenrolled banks attempting screening raise `NotParticipantError(ValueError)`, mapped directly to HTTP 403 Forbidden without brittle string matching.
- Explicit application factory: `create_app` strictly requires `clock`, `key_provider`, and `settings` without implicit defaults; `create_app_prod` alone binds to `WallClock`, environment variable `NATIONALSIM_DPIP_KEY`, and `config/nationalsim.yaml`.
