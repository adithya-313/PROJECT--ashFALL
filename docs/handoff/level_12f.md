## Decisions and why
- Test worlds use the TRAINING ranges (report Section 6), not world_ranges.yaml's wider test column, which is frozen
  and stays unused by sampled worlds. A world's split (which tactics it may hold) is separate from its range column
  (which numbers it draws): run_interactive_world takes world_split, and the runner refuses any pairing other than
  training/validation/test on the training column and stress on the stress column. world_split enters the run id
  only when set, so no earlier run id moved.
- The Latin hypercube sets the CENTRE of each gang-level setting per world; each gang draws around it (spread 0.15 of
  the range width). World-level settings are pinned per world in a private config copy. Complaint delay is never
  pinned; it stays a per-complaint draw.
- Every world gets a generated gang mix (2 to 4 gangs), because a single fixture gang in every world would vary the
  labels but never the fraud.
- Worlds come in blocks of 40, each its own hypercube, so Level 18 can follow the learning-curve rule in A-023 (b).
- Counts (A-023): training 320, validation 120, test 120, stress 120, sized from measured labels (about 5 true
  complaints and 20 true mule accounts per 61-day world) and measured run time (about 1.9 h on 4 workers, byte-identical
  to serial).
- Stress worlds run as long as their longest ranged event (120 days today), from horizon_limits in config.
- Plugins may only spend time and money; they cannot create payments, roles or device events, which enforces the
  device-retirement dividing line by construction. The AST lint is not a sandbox; plugins are local code the owner
  writes.
- Pattern preview counts money taken in once (money entering the mule network from outside); the first version counted
  every hop, about 1.97 times too much.

## Bugs found and fixed in earlier parts
The generated gang mix was the first code to send gang-opened accounts through cash-out. Three 12c/12d bugs sat on that
path, invisible because every fixture used rented mules and one terminal mule:
1. bddeeb8: opening handles were not resolved in cash withdrawals and account resales.
2. b81c40e: withdrawals from accounts opened mid-run were refused before the run started.
3. cc47a67: the crypto collector could open after its first payment (terminal mules are not in time order).
Each has a regression test, and the pinned plan hashes and run ids did not move. Lesson for later parts: test through
the generator and the sweep, not only through hand-made fixtures. scripts/measure_world_block.py --sweep is the tool.

## Stress worlds with no complaints
In the stress block, all three 2-gang worlds had 0 true complaints. The measured cause, from the commit 15 check:
All three had `true_complaints == 0`. I reran each with the same spec through `run_sampled_world`. Every world ended at minute 172,799 (120 days), and every would-be complaint would have arrived before then, so delays are not the cause.

| world | victims | successful victim payments | losses the planner sees | share drawn | cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stress-b00-0003 | 12 | 14 | 10 | 0.1569 | none of the 10 complain draws is below 0.1569 (lowest 0.3403) |
| stress-b00-0007 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 0.6458 | one loss, draw 0.8311 is above the share |
| stress-b00-0008 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 0.4458 | all three draws (0.8141 to 0.8741) are above the share |

- **FACT, would-be delays and arrivals:** Would-be delays (minutes) in world 0003, by loss: 81,345, 31,828, 1,302, 25,782, 74,072, 26,688, 33,054, 670, 17,921 and 22,261. In world 0007 it is 3,447, and in world 0008 it is 34,560, 4,218 and 1,281. Each would arrive well before minute 172,799.
- **FACT:** The complaint planner draws each loss's "complains?" and "delay" uniforms, and a loss complains only when its draw is below the world's share. The ranges and the planner behave as designed.
- **ESTIMATE:** This is the ranges and small samples, not a bug. For world 0003 the chance that all 10 losses stay silent at a share of 0.1569 is about 18%. The other two worlds have 1 and 3 losses.
- **FACT:** The builder's total of 3, 6 and 12 label events in those worlds is false complaints only. Their true count is 0.
