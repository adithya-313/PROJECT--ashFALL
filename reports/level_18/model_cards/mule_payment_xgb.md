# Model card: mule_payment_xgb

SIMULATED. Every number below is copied from the committed Level 18 console files and describes simulated data, not real customers.

## Intended use

Rank same-bank incoming payments for human review at the frozen hold share 0.005 and the committed threshold, using the frozen xgboost payment model recorded in payment_results.json (647 boosted rounds, seed 1717).

Not intended for:

- Blocking payments automatically or any decision without a human reviewer
- Ranking payments whose receiver is at another bank, which carry no payee-risk score
- Production evidence while leakage checks id_leak and 6b are recorded as failed
- Payments from banks or behaviours outside the simulator's rules

## Training data

Label: **SIMULATED**. Payment rows generated by the ashFALL interactive simulator. The frozen payment model is scored without re-training; the test check uses the hidden receiver role as truth (A-060a).

## Known limitations

- Per-payee precision is 0.911046, not the reported per-world median 0.994179: 1023 distinct payees and 11196 held payments, so the per-world number overstates precision at the payee level.
- Payment ranking collapses under stress: test truth AP median 0.632033 falls to 0.070938, while the payee-risk feature alone keeps 0.543550 under stress against 0.922653 on test.
- Payees at another bank have no payee score: 0.617159 of test payments carry a NaN payee risk, and receiver_at_other_bank accounts for 1115957 of the NaN rows.
- Simulator new-account cleanness: the simulator opens accounts itself and records the opening minute (world/bank_connectors.py:344-369), so a new account here has no prior history; a mule's fresh account opened with a borrowed identity is not modelled, so new-account detection is cleaner than reality.
- Capacity rounds to one per bank-day: recall at capacity uses max(1, ceil(active_accounts * capacity_pct / 100)) per bank-day (evaluation/level17_scoring.py:527), so on a bank-day with few active accounts a single review decision is the whole capacity.
